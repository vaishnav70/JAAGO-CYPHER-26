PROTOTYPE THRESHOLDS (NOT CLINICAL)
  • Normal blink: 100–400ms (not counted as drowsy)
  • Distraction trigger: Yaw > 25° OR Pitch > 20° (sustained)
  • Distraction exit: Yaw < 12° AND Pitch < 10° for ≥ 0.8s
  • Distracted Level 1: ≥ 1.2s continuous look-away (min 3s sound)
  • Distracted Level 2: ≥ 3.0s continuous look-away (escalating loop)
  • Lost-face hold: 2.0s hold before pausing (never defaults to Focused)
  • Level 3 drowsy: > 1.5s sustained eye closure (adjustable)
  • Yawn detection: > 1.5s sustained jaw opening
